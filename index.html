About

Economics taught me to ask why. Data science taught me how to test it.

My path began with a BSc (Hons.) in Economics and a minor in Mathematics, followed by public-health research and a growing interest in analytical methods. I completed a Master of Data Science at the University of British Columbia, where I worked across predictive analytics, collaborative software development, and reproducible research.

Today I bring those disciplines together: framing the right question, building the technical system, and communicating what the evidence means.
